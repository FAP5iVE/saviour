Phase 10 (showcase: no more scroll-lock; scroll bar; section rail is now a 3D wheel), Sat 7 Nov 2026.
Unzip the WHOLE zip over your copy, keeping the paths (js, css and index files all changed together).
Do NOT delete anything: showcase-horizontal.js and showcase-dial.js are now tiny pass-through files, so
an older index.js that still imports either of them cannot break the page.

FIXED:    On desktop the page used to stop at the Business showcase until you had scrolled all six
          panels sideways, and on a short screen the pinned section was cut off at the bottom. The
          section is not pinned any more: the page scrolls straight through it.
SHOWCASE: The wheel is gone from the showcase. A custom scroll bar sits under the row instead: a slim
          track with a blue thumb (orange while you drag it) and a notch for each project, plus a
          01 / 06 counter. Drag the thumb, click the track to jump to a project, use the arrow keys
          (Home and End too) when the bar has focus, or swipe sideways on a trackpad. The row settles
          on a project after every move. Up and down mouse-wheel scrolling is never captured.
RAIL:     The vertical rail on the right edge is now the 3D wheel: a scale of fine ticks with a tall
          numbered tick for each of the 8 sections, drawn on a drum so it bunches up, shrinks and
          fades toward the top and bottom (bulged). The drum turns as you scroll, and the section
          you are in sits under a fixed orange needle with a round head. No background: the ticks
          take the colour of the page behind (dark on light sections, white on dark ones).
PHONES:   Unchanged (native swipe row with dots, no rail).
