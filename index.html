About us

Customers look for businesses on their phones. A business that can't be found online, or only shows up as scattered posts, loses people before they ever call. Many small businesses and new entrepreneurs in Malawi don't have the time, money or technical help to fix that. That is the gap we exist to close.

  • In 2019, almost

    1.6 million

    micro, small and medium businesses were operating in Malawi.

    Source: 2019 FinScope MSME Survey, as reported by the World Bank (opens in a new tab), 2022.

Who we are

Saviour Digital Solutions is a new team in Blantyre. We build websites, do data collection and help businesses get noticed online, mainly for small businesses and new entrepreneurs. We are also glad to work with individuals, start-ups, NGOs and other organisations.

How we started

Our founder, Saviour Kumalawi, took part in the Zantchito Entrepreneurship Incubation Programme, which covered business development and managing money. One lesson, in digital and entrepreneurship skills from Rynberg Stacks, sparked a hunger to learn more, and to turn what we learned into something bigger: a business that gives clients the best all-round digital solutions we can, right here in Blantyre.

Yes, the name is a pun. It is also our founder's name, so there was no getting out of it. With a name like Saviour, we had to be of some use: finding small businesses that are lost online and helping customers find them. We want to go big, and we are starting at home.

Questions

Short answers to read before you ask for a quote.

Ask your own question on WhatsApp

  • How long does a website take?

    It depends on the package and on how soon you send us your photos and details. Ask us and we will tell you for your site.

  • What do I need to give you?

    Your business name, your logo if you have one, photos, a list of your products or services, and your contact details.

  • How do I pay?

    By Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba or bank transfer. You can pay in instalments. We agree a payment plan in your written quote.

  • Do you register domain names or host websites?

    No. We do not register domain names and we do not offer hosting. If you are not sure how a finished site goes live, ask us before you decide.

  • What happens after the site goes live?

    Maintenance and support is one of our services: updates, backups, small content changes and fixes when something breaks. Ask about it when you request a quote.

  • Can I change things later?

    Yes. Small content changes are part of our maintenance and support service. For bigger changes, message us and we will tell you what is involved.
