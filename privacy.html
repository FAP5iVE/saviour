Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This page explains what we do with the information you give us on this website. We write it in plain words. If anything is unclear, ask us.

Who we are

Saviour Digital Solutions is a business in Blantyre, Malawi. We build websites and offer digital services to small businesses and new entrepreneurs. We decide why and how the information on this page is used, so we are responsible for it under Malawi's Data Protection Act, 2024.

You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp on +265 886 02 34 82, or by email at saviourkumalawi@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send the quote form, we collect what you type into it:

  • your name
  • your phone or WhatsApp number
  • your email address, if you give one
  • the package and add-ons you choose
  • your budget and your message, if you write them

We also record which button on the website brought you to the form (for example a package or a service), and the date and time you agreed to be contacted.

If you message or call us on WhatsApp or by phone instead of using the form, we see your number and what you tell us. WhatsApp and your phone company handle those messages under their own rules.

Why we use it

We use it only to reply to your request, send you a written quote and contact you about it. We ask for your consent before you send the form: you tick a box to say you agree.

Who else handles it

  • Web3Forms (web3forms.com (opens in a new tab)) is the form service. It receives what you type and sends it to us by email. Its own privacy information on its website explains what it keeps.
  • The company that hosts this website serves the pages to you. Like any host, it may keep technical records of visits, such as your IP address.
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to message us there.

Cookies

We do not use cookies to track you, and we do not run advertising or tracking tools on this website. The fonts and pictures come from our own website.

How long we keep it

We keep your request only for as long as we need it to reply to you, send a quote and look after any work we do for you.

Your rights

Under Malawi's Data Protection Act, 2024 you can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop or limit how we use it

You can also take back your consent at any time. That does not change what we did before you told us. To do any of this, call or message us on +265 886 02 34 82 or email saviourkumalawi@gmail.com.

If you are not happy

Tell us first, so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA), which supervises the Data Protection Act, 2024.

Changes to this page

If we change this policy, we will change the date at the top of the page.
